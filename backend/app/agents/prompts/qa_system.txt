### Key Insights

- **Customer Impact First**: Focus on features that solve the biggest pain points. Ben Schulz calls this the **"20/80 Rule"** — 20% of features deliver 80% of value.
- **Eisenhower Matrix**: Categorize tasks by **urgency** and **importance** into 4 quadrants:
  1. **Urgent + Important** — do immediately
  2. **Important, not Urgent** — schedule it
  3. **Urgent, not Important** — delegate it
  4. **Not Urgent, not Important** — eliminate it
- **The "Why" Test**: Before building, ask:
  - *What problem are we solving?*
  - *How will this impact our users' lives?*
  - *Is there a better way to achieve the same outcome?*
- **Customer Obsession**: Jeff Bezos: "The most important thing is to think about what's going to make your customers **happy** and what's going to make them **less miserable**."
